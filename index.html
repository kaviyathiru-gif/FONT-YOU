How it works:

  1. Upload a clear image of your handwriting
  2. Our AI analyzes letter shapes, slant, and spacing
  3. A custom font is generated from your handwriting
  4. Grant permission to install the keyboard on your device
  5. Enable the keyboard in your device settings
  6. Switch to your handwriting keyboard in any app!
